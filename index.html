Veterans Day Guest Speaker
Featured guest

Judge E. Faye
Peterson

Callaway Class of 1982Former JROTC CadetHinds County Circuit Court

Judge E. Faye Peterson is a lifelong Jackson resident, a 1982 graduate of Callaway High School, and a former JROTC cadet. She earned a B.S. in Physics from Jackson State University and attended Mississippi College School of Law.

Her career has centered on public service. In 2001, she became Mississippi’s first African American woman appointed—and later elected—as a district attorney. Elected to the Hinds County Circuit Court in 2018, she has expanded public access through virtual proceedings, livestreamed court sessions, and diversion programs. Her journey from Callaway JROTC to the bench reflects leadership, service, and perseverance.
